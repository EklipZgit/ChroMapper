# Missing lightmaps and reflection probes in generated environments

## What we observed

At beat 373.187 in DELETE IT ALL (ExpertPlus Standard, InterscopeEnvironment), the two bright top panels are `Pillar (2)/NeonLightL` and `NeonLightR` BoxLights. Their sibling `PillarL`/`PillarR` meshes exist in CM, but the dim housing visible in game was nearly black. In CM's `Assets/__Scenes/Environments/InterscopeEnvironment.unity`, `BakedLightDataLoader.LightmapData` and `BakedReflectionProbe.ReflectionProbeData` are both null. The scene generator adds a `BakedLightDataLoader` (`Assets/Editor/Environments/Create/03_BuildComponents.cs`) without populating its data; CM's loader then binds 1×1 black `_LightMap1`/`_LightMap2` textures. `Interscope/Pillar.mat` enables `LIGHTMAP` but **not** probe reflection; `EnvLightGlass.mat` enables probe reflection, and some Interscope concrete materials enable both. These are distinct contributions.

This is not necessarily isolated: 41 of the 42 generated environment scenes currently have a null `LightmapData` reference, but a null loader only matters where a *used* renderer has a lightmap-enabled material. Billie, Skrillex, and Linkin Park are confirmed candidates: their generated scenes reference lightmapped materials and have null loaders, while their exported game scenes reference lightmap data. Audit individual renderers, not just the loader count.

**Failed experiment—do not repeat as a fix:** We copied the two Interscope PNGs from a Beat Saber 1.44.1 AssetRipper export, bound them in CM, and made the housing visible, but a live Unity screenshot showed severe, incorrect magenta illumination across the walls. We removed the binding, imported assets, and approach-specific regression test. The GUID chain identified the source assets; it did **not** prove CM decoded, sampled, or composed them as the game does. A separately rebaked CM reflection probe did not fix the housing and added an unwanted gray floor; that bake was also reverted. Neither experiment established a visually correct asset import.

## Recover the *game's* asset references

The installed 1.44.1 game bundles include `interscopeenvironment_scenes_all_*.bundle` and `interscopeenvironment_assets_all_*.bundle` under `Beat Saber_Data/StreamingAssets/aa/StandaloneWindows64`. The existing AssetRipper export is under `C:/src/BeatSaberStuff/BeatSaberDecompilation/1.44.1/SerializedAssets/AssetRipperOutput/ExportedProject/Assets`. Treat its PNG conversions and metadata as leads to verify against the bundles, not authoritative GPU output.

1. In `Packages/com.beatgames.beatsaber.environments.interscope/Scenes/InterscopeEnvironment.unity`, identify the original `BakedLightDataLoader._lightmapData` GUID (`eb9e743ac24af8a469c383b083089492`) and `BakedReflectionProbe._reflectionProbeData` GUID (`54056d0d1cfdadc4da451406c275a734`). Resolve each through exported `.asset.meta` files, then follow the two texture GUIDs from each data asset to their texture files. For the lightmap asset, the two texture GUIDs are `c49c8c9d45aa0df43afdce8460c7f279` and `a43fe6635c43aa84a90058ea3c2e7841` (exported as `Texture2D/Lightmap_0-0_comp_light_1.png` and `Lightmap_1-0_comp_light_1.png`). Resolve the probe's *own* two cubemap GUIDs separately; do not replace them with a newly baked CM probe.
2. Compare the exported textures with the corresponding original bundle objects: dimensions, format/HDR range, color-space/sRGB handling, mipmaps, channel packing, and orientation. Determine whether the PNG export or Unity re-import changes encoded lightmap values. Preserve the game's A–F channel-to-bake-ID mapping for both lightmaps and the packed probe pair; a matching filename or GUID is not a rendered-parity test.
3. Check the *receiving geometry* as well: `PillarL/R` and nearby concrete mesh UV2, renderer lightmap scale/offset, material keywords, and the `_LightmapLightBakeIdA`–`F` shader globals at the reported beat. Compare the game's material/shader output and CM's `Assets/_Graphics/Shaders/Lit.shader` lightmap decode, fog, exposure, and color-space conversion. A correct atlas sampled with the wrong UV/scale, channel colors, or decode can light the entire scene incorrectly. Identify the actual mismatch before binding textures globally again.
4. For reflection probes, compare the game's `ReflectionProbeDataSO` cubemap pair, bounds, and `_LightProbeLightBakeIdA`–`F` globals with CM's `BakedReflectionProbe` and `ShaderLibrary/Common/Reflection.hlsl`. `EnvLightGlass` and some concrete use probe data; `Pillar` does not. Verify each contribution separately instead of assuming missing probes alone explain the pillar housing.

## Integrate and verify safely

- Add an Editor validation pass over generated environment scenes: when an *assigned renderer material* enables `LIGHTMAP`, require a loader with both usable textures; when it enables `REFLECTION_PROBE`, check the scene's probe data and both cubemaps. Report the scene, renderer ID, and material, and distinguish intentionally unused loaders. The generator should resolve known per-environment data assets or issue a clear warning rather than silently shipping black fallbacks. Exercise loaded Unity objects in tests; do not assert source/YAML tokens.
- First write a behavioral regression using the real map at beat 373.187: sample housing and surrounding wall render output or material/UV/light state, including a nearby unaffected surface. Demonstrate the expected failure *before* a production change. Capture same-camera/game-setting A/B images at that beat, compare against the provided in-game reference, then check earlier beats and reverse seeks. A batch `Camera.Render()` capture is useful diagnostic evidence but is **not** the composited live Unity Game view.
- Test lightmaps only, original probes only, and both together; keep the current no-asset render as baseline. Reject any trial that restores the small housing but makes distant walls or floors wrong. Import only verified texture formats and link only the relevant scene; avoid a global material tint or guessed fallback. Run `pwsh ./Assets/Tests/Test-ChroMapper.ps1 -TestFilter '<Fully.Qualified.TestName>'` from this worktree when its exact project path is not open in Unity. Do not build/deploy ChroMapper for testing.
- Beware `Assets/Editor/Environments/ReflectionProbeBake/ReflectionProbeBakePipeline.cs`: an earlier probe bake opened/saved the generated scene and serialized many unrelated light-component runtime fields. Before any new bake, protect the original scene, restore captured component state, and inspect the complete scene diff. If a visual attempt fails, remove its asset/scene changes rather than leaving a green test for an incorrect picture.
